# DevFest Berlin 2026 Vibe Lounge

Build a working app with Gemini in under an hour. Guided sprints at DevFest Berlin 2026, Saturday 14 November,
SRH Berlin, 11:00 to 15:00. Led by [Esra Kadah](https://github.com/esrakadah).

Event page: [2026.devfest-berlin.de/vibe-lounge](https://2026.devfest-berlin.de/vibe-lounge/)

Sprints start at 11:00, 13:00 and 14:00; come at the start of one. No lounge over lunch (11:45 to 13:00).

## Challenges

All four run in every sprint, and they are free for every DevFest attendee. Pick whichever you like, or bring your own
idea. Each one is sized for a single sprint.

1. **The Backyard Barista.** A Gemini barista that picks beans, roasts and milk for your taste, then a brew
   timer, a shopping list and a Firebase recipe journal.
   [Challenge guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12Vvc5VPJkHvRzgMgcHdvMFDzxsWtfBZzzoo0Y9HM_8M/edit?tab=t.0)
2. **Launch your portfolio website with AI.** Describe yourself, let AI Studio build the site, publish it to Cloud Run.
   [Codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/launch-your-portfolio-website-with-ai)
3. **Launch a multiplayer game with AI.** A turn-based browser game with Firestore and Google Sign-In, deployed to
   Cloud Run. [Codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/launch-your-web-based-video-game-with-ai)
4. **Build with Google Maps Platform and AI.** A map explorer with geocoding, Gemini local facts and Maps grounding.
   [Codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/build-with-google-maps-platform-and-ai)

## What to bring

- A personal laptop. A phone works, but a laptop makes switching between preview, code and prompts much easier. Work
  laptops often block what AI Studio needs.
- A personal Google account (an @gmail.com address). Work and university accounts can block features or deployment.
- Your charger.

## How a sprint works

1. Check in at the door at the start of the sprint.
2. Open your challenge and sign in at [ai.studio/build](https://ai.studio/build).
3. Start from the challenge prompt, then add one feature per prompt. Mentors in the room help when you get stuck.
4. Publish to Cloud Run from AI Studio, or use Share for a quick public link. With a personal account and no Google
   Cloud billing history, the free Starter Tier covers up to two Cloud Run projects.
5. A few people demo their live apps, and everyone can claim the DevFest 2026 Vibe Lounge badge in the room.
