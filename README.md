# DevFest Berlin 2026 Vibe Lounge

Come build an app with Gemini! 🚀

Saturday 14 November, 11:00 to 15:00, at SRH Berlin University of Applied Sciences. 📍

Bring your laptop, pick a challenge, and leave with an app you can share. ✨

The Vibe Lounge is a relaxed room for building with Google AI Studio and Gemini. 🤍
No coding experience needed. Every challenge starts simple, and you can keep going if you want more. 💪

Hosted by [Esra Kadah](https://github.com/esrakadah), Google Developer Expert for Flutter & Dart, with community
mentors in the room.

Event page: [2026.devfest-berlin.de/vibe-lounge](https://2026.devfest-berlin.de/vibe-lounge/)

## When to come

Sprints start at 11:00, 13:00 and 14:00. Come at the start of one, so you build together with the room. ⏰
The lounge takes a lunch break from 11:45 to 13:00. 🥪

| Sprint | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11:00 to 11:45 | Morning build: pick any of the four challenges and build it with Google AI Studio and Gemini. 🚀 |
| 2 | 13:00 to 14:00 | After-lunch build: same four challenges, fresh start. Mentors in the room help you get unstuck. 🙌 |
| 3 | 14:00 to 15:00 | Build and show: build any challenge, then show your app in the last demo round of the day. 🥳 |

## Pick a challenge

All four run in every sprint, and they are free for every DevFest attendee. 🤩
Pick the one you like, or bring your own idea. Each one is sized for a single sprint. 😉

1. **The Backyard Barista.** A Gemini barista that picks beans, roasts and milk for your taste. Then add a brew timer,
   a shopping list and a Firebase recipe journal. The guide takes about 40 minutes. ☕
   [Open the Barista guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12Vvc5VPJkHvRzgMgcHdvMFDzxsWtfBZzzoo0Y9HM_8M/edit?tab=t.0)
2. **Launch your portfolio website with AI.** Tell Google AI Studio about yourself, let it build your portfolio site,
   then publish it to Cloud Run. ✨
   [Open the portfolio codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/launch-your-portfolio-website-with-ai)
3. **Launch a multiplayer game with AI.** A turn-based browser game with Firestore and Google Sign-In, set up for you
   by AI Studio. Play it with friends on Cloud Run. 🎮
   [Open the game codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/launch-your-web-based-video-game-with-ai)
4. **Build with Google Maps Platform and AI.** A map explorer with geocoding, local facts from Gemini and Maps
   grounding, deployed to Cloud Run. 🗺️
   [Open the Maps codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-run/build-with-google-maps-platform-and-ai)

## What to bring

- Your own laptop. A phone works too, but switching between preview, code and prompts is much easier on a laptop.
  Work laptops often block what AI Studio needs. 💻
- A personal Google account (an @gmail.com address). Work and university accounts can block features or
  deployment. 🔑
- Your phone, to open your app and share it. 📱
- Your charger. 🔌

Tip: sign in at [ai.studio/build](https://ai.studio/build) with your personal account before you come, so your sprint
starts with building. 😉

## How a sprint works

1. Come in at the start of a sprint. A volunteer at the door checks you in and points you to a seat. 👋
2. Open your challenge from this page and sign in at [ai.studio/build](https://ai.studio/build). 💻
3. Start from the challenge prompt, then add one feature per prompt. Stuck? Mentors are right there to help. 🤓
4. Want to publish it? Deploy to Cloud Run from AI Studio, or use Share for a quick public link. With a personal account and
   no Google Cloud billing history, the free Starter Tier covers up to two Cloud Run projects. 🚀
5. Show it and claim your badge. At the end of the sprint a few people demo their apps, and everyone can claim the
   official DevFest 2026 Vibe Lounge badge in the room. 🥳
